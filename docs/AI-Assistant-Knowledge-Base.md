# Dialect Library Help

Dialect Library is a platform where verified contributors help train AI in local dialects. Trainers complete voice and word-training tasks, then receive DL according to approved task results.

## Account and onboarding

- Create an account, verify your email, choose your country of origin and dialect, then complete onboarding before starting training.
- Account profile and phone verification are available at [Profile](/dashboard/profile).
- Never ask a user for a password, OTP, recovery code, wallet key, or payment card details.

## Training

- Start a word-training task from [Training](/dashboard/training).
- Read or pronounce the prompt clearly, provide the requested spelling or translation, review the recording, then submit it.
- A task may be skipped when a word is unfamiliar or cannot be translated confidently.
- Submitted work is scored after validation. Scores and submitted tasks are visible in [My Scores](/dashboard/scores) and [Training](/dashboard/training).

## Tokens and earnings

- Token balances and account activity are shown in [Tokens](/dashboard/tokens).
- Training payouts and referral bonuses are shown in [Earnings](/dashboard/earnings).
- Current rates, withdrawal availability, and eligibility can change under platform settings. Do not promise a payout amount or timeframe.

## Referrals and market

- Referral information is available from Profile after signing in.
- The P2P market may be disabled by the administrator. When enabled, it is available at [Market](/dashboard/market).
- Never advise a user to pay outside the trade workflow or to release tokens before confirming payment.

## Learning and updates

- Platform courses are available at [Courses](/courses).
- Blog updates are available at [Blog](/blog).
- Notices and account updates appear in [Notifications](/notifications).

## Support boundaries

- For account access, payment, scoring, or safety problems that are not answered here, direct the user to platform support.
- The assistant cannot change account settings, approve payments, resolve disputes, or access private account data.
